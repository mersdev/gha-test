31 August 1957

Hari Merdeka

Malaysia's Independence Day

What is it?

Hari Merdeka (Independence Day) commemorates the day the Federation of Malaya gained independence from British colonial rule on 31 August 1957. It marks the end of over a century of British administration and the birth of a self-governing nation in Southeast Asia.

The Historic Moment

At the stroke of midnight on 31 August 1957, in front of a huge crowd at the Merdeka Stadium in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia's first Prime Minister, Tunku Abdul Rahman, led the crowd in chanting "Merdeka!" (Freedom!) seven times — one of the most iconic moments in Malaysian history.

Important Note

Hari Merdeka celebrates the independence of Malaya (Peninsular Malaysia only) in 1957 — six years before Malaysia itself was formed. This is different from Malaysia Day (16 September), which marks the formation of the larger federation including Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore.

How It's Celebrated

  • National flag hoisting ceremonies nationwide
  • Grand parades led by the Prime Minister
  • Fireworks and cultural performances
  • Houses and streets decorated with the Jalur Gemilang (flag)
